We report an initial search for ultralight dark matter using a magnetically levitated particle, suspending a sub-millimeter permanent magnet in a superconducting trap and achieving a measured force sensitivity of 0.2 fN/√Hz. Finding no signal, we set constraints on dark matter coupled to the difference between baryon and lepton number (B-L) over the mass range (1.10360-1.10485) x 10^-13 eV/c², with our most stringent coupling limit g(B-L) ≲ 2.98 x 10^-21, and outline the planned POLONAISE experiment for further sensitivity gains."
