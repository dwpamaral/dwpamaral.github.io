We examine how a gauged U(1)_{L_mu-L_tau} model addresses the muon anomalous magnetic moment tension with Standard Model predictions, confirmed at 4.2 sigma, and propose combining data from different future probes to identify the nature of the new physics, contrasting our model with an effective U(1)_{L_mu} alternative. Fixed-target experiments like NA64mu, coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering detectors, and dark matter experiments such as DARWIN could provide complementary measurements of the hidden photon's coupling and mass."
