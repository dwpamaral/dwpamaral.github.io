We propose that quantum sensor arrays can detect ultraheavy dark matter with finite spatial extent, since particles at the Planck scale cannot be elementary and must be composite. We model the gravitational and Yukawa interactions of such extended objects with detector arrays for tophat, Gaussian, and exponential density profiles, comparing signals against point-particle models via Monte Carlo simulations that include thermal and quantum noise. We find a non-trivial interplay between the dark-matter scale radius, inter-sensor spacing, and Yukawa screening length, showing accelerometer arrays can probe both the mass and size of ultraheavy dark matter candidates."
