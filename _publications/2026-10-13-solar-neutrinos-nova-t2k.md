A tension between NOvA and T2K data can be explained by vector non-standard neutrino interactions (NSI) with complex couplings, which introduce degeneracies in long-baseline measurements. We show that dark matter direct detection experiments, using solar neutrino data from LZ, XENONnT, and PandaX-4T, already constrain part of this NSI solution and can probe regions of parameter space that even DUNE will be blind to. Our analysis combines nuclear recoils from <sup>8</sup>B neutrinos and electron recoils from <i>pp</i> neutrinos, and a future xenon experiment such as XLZD or PandaX-xT will cover a significant fraction of the NSI solution of the NOvA-T2K tension."
