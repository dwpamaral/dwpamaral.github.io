We explore using mechanical sensors to detect dark matter candidates near the Planck mass scale, employing statistical track-finding methods to identify signatures across sensor arrays under various experimental configurations. While purely gravitational detection would demand extreme sensitivity (~80 dB of quantum noise reduction with a 100^3 array), we identify numerous unexplored dark matter candidates that couple through long-range forces and are accessible with current or near-term technology."
