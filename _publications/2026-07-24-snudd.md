We present SNuDD, an open-source Python package for computing the solar neutrino rate spectrum at direct detection experiments, covering both nuclear and electron recoils under the Standard Model and under beyond-Standard-Model neutral-current non-standard interactions (NSI). Using data from LZ, XENONnT, and PandaX-4T, we derive new constraints on effective NSI couplings and project future sensitivities, showing that direct detection experiments are closing in on the reach of dedicated neutrino observatories."
