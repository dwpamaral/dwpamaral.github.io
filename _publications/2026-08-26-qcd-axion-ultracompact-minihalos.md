excerpt: "Draft blurb — edit me! We explore how ultracompact minihalos of QCD-axion dark matter, seeded by stellar-mass primordial black holes, produce detectable multi-messenger signals. Using numerical modeling based on secondary-infall frameworks, we characterize the halo structure and examine signatures from neutron-star-primordial-black-hole interactions: dynamical friction causes gravitational-wave dephasing detectable by LISA for primordial black holes below about 10 solar masses, while gravitationally focused axions can resonantly convert into photons in the neutron-star magnetosphere, producing a narrow-band radio signature detectable by the Green Bank telescope in the microelectronvolt mass range from nearby galactic neutron stars — with the combined gravitational-wave and radio signals providing distinctive evidence for a mixed primordial-black-hole-and-axion dark sector."
