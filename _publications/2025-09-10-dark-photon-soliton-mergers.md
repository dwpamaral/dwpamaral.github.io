We place initial constraints on dark photon solitonic dark matter using the absence of expected radio-frequency signals from soliton mergers near supermassive black holes, developing a novel merger-rate calculation that incorporates dense dark matter density profiles and velocity dispersions. Comparing our predictions against fast radio burst survey observations yields constraints on the solitonic dark matter fraction and dark-visible coupling strength, establishing astrophysical transients as powerful probes of dark sectors and opening new pathways to detecting ultralight vector fields."
