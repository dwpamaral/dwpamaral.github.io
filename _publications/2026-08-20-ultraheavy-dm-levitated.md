We report the first search for ultraheavy dark matter using a milligram-scale magnetic particle suspended in a superconducting trap, sensitive to forces as small as 0.07 fN/√Hz and momentum transfers down to 1 TeV/c. We set constraints on dark matter-neutron interactions across masses from 10^6 to 10^15 GeV/c², excluding couplings as low as 3.2×10^-9 for light mediators, extending levitated-sensor technology into the ultraheavy dark matter regime."
