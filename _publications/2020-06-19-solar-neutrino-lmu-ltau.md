Gauged U(1)_{L_mu-L_tau} models can resolve the muon anomalous magnetic moment discrepancy via a new light vector mediator, which also helps alleviate an existing tension in the determination of the Hubble parameter. We investigate this solution using solar neutrino scattering data, finding that Borexino constraints exclude mediator masses below 2x10^-2 GeV, and show that future direct dark matter detectors such as LUX-ZEPLIN and DARWIN will be able to probe most of the remaining parameter space up to and beyond 10^-1 GeV."
