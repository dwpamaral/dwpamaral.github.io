We tackle the challenge of exploring high-dimensional parameter spaces common in beyond-Standard-Model physics, using GPU acceleration, automatic differentiation, and neural-network-guided reparameterization to speed up Bayesian inference for dark matter direct detection experiments probing neutrino non-standard interactions. Our approach is roughly 60-100 times faster than traditional nested sampling, enabling the first comprehensive scan allowing simultaneous variation of all neutrino NSI parameters, with techniques applicable more broadly to multi-dimensional parameter exploration in astroparticle physics."
