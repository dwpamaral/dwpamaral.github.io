We examine how direct detection experiments can probe non-standard neutrino interactions (NSI) through solar neutrino scattering, providing a complementary view of the NSI landscape to that of spallation sources and neutrino oscillation experiments. We develop a new parametrization framework and the SNuDD software to analyze data from XENONnT and LUX-ZEPLIN, along with projections for future detectors, showing next-generation direct detection experiments will become important tools for studying neutrino NSI."
