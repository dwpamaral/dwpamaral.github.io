We address concerns that primordial black holes (PBHs) as a complete dark matter explanation would generate excessive fast radio burst (FRB) rates, recalculating these rates with improved velocity distribution computations and by accounting for density enhancements near supermassive black holes. We find previous estimates of the FRB rate sourced by PBH capture by neutron stars were three orders of magnitude too large, suggesting the PBH-as-dark-matter hypothesis remains scientifically viable."
