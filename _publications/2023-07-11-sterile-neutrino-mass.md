We investigate how direct detection and spallation source experiments complement each other in studying sterile neutrino physics, considering an extension of the Standard Model that introduces a massive sterile neutrino coupled to the quark sector via a new gauge boson. Using COHERENT collaboration data, we derive new constraints and show that future experiments could reconstruct sterile neutrino masses between 15-50 MeV, with direct detection experiments improving precision to roughly 9 MeV by resolving degeneracies in the mixing parameters."
