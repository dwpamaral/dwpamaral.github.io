We investigate how ultralight spin-1 particles (dark photons) could constitute dark matter and produce detectable signals in terrestrial quantum sensors, developing a framework that accounts for the stochastic, vector nature of the field and Earth's rotational effects. We identify a characteristic three-peak Fourier signature arising from the vector field tracing an ellipse, enabling latitude-independent constraints on such dark matter couplings, and demonstrate our approach on searches for ultralight B-L dark matter using optomechanical sensors, exploring previously unexplored parameter space."
