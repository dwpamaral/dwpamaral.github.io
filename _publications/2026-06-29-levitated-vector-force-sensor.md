We describe a magnetically levitated ferromagnetic sensor for spin-dependent exotic interactions from beyond-Standard-Model bosons, mapping forces along different axes onto distinct translational modes with different resonance frequencies to separate interaction channels. As a demonstration, we consider parity-violating axial-vector interactions mediated by a Z' boson between two levitated ferromagnets, showing the setup can probe the pure electron-electron coupling g_A^e g_V^e in a previously inaccessible sub-centimeter force range, corresponding to mediator masses above 10^-5 eV/c², making this platform promising for millimeter-scale searches for spin-dependent fifth forces."
