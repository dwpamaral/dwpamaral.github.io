I am a theoretical physicist working at the interface of astroparticle physics, quantum sensing, and precision measurement. My research develops new experimental probes of dark matter and physics beyond the Standard Model, with particular emphasis on levitated sensors and other quantum technologies. My work spans ultralight and ultraheavy dark matter, fifth forces, neutrino physics, and high-frequency gravitational waves.


## Academic Positions

### Postdoctoral Researcher, IFAE, Barcelona, Spain (2025 -- present)
Developing theoretical frameworks and phenomenology for high-frequency
gravitational-wave detection using electromagnetic cavities.

### Postdoctoral Researcher, Rice University, Texas, USA (2022 -- 2025)
Led the first search for ultralight dark matter using a levitated sensor,
worked on ultralight dark matter phenomenology with quantum sensors, and
applied machine learning techniques to neutrino physics at direct detection
experiments.

### PhD, Durham University, UK (2018 -- 2022)
Explored how dark matter direct detection experiments can be re-imagined as sensitive neutrino observatories. Led to development of the SNuDD codebase <a href="https://github.com/SNuDD/SNuDD"><i class="fab fa-github" aria-hidden="true"></i></a>.

Thesis: [_A New Era for Direct Detection: Probing New Neutrino Physics at Dark Matter Detectors with Solar Neutrinos_](https://etheses.durham.ac.uk/id/eprint/14853/)
